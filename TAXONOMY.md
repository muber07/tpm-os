# TPM OS Taxonomy

## Portfolio
A collection of Programs managed by the TPM.

Examples: FinProd Lending, FinProd Foundation, Earner Products

---

## Program
A top-level initiative with its own PRD, milestone map, launch date, and owner set.

Each program has one `PROGRAM_STATE.yaml`.

Examples:
- US Earner Lending (in-house)
- PCP (Pending Cash Plus)
- Merchant Lending
- UK Earner Lending

---

## Workstream
An execution track within a Program. Owned by a specific eng or cross-functional team. Has its own milestone, risks, and dependencies.

Examples (US Earner Lending):
- Compliance (KYC, MLA, OFAC, AML)
- Bancorp (partner bank)
- Identity / IDVP (selfie check)
- Payments (Tabapay ACH/RTP)
- UUE (Uber Underwriting Engine)
- FMS (Financing Management System)
- Data Engineering
- Testing / E2E
- Product / Entrypoint
- Risk & Fraud

---

## Agent
A reusable reasoning engine. Operates across all programs and workstreams. Each agent has a defined purpose, invokes specific skills, and produces a defined output.

Agents do not store state — they read from PROGRAM_STATE.yaml and write back to it.

8 agents total. See AGENTS/ directory.

---

## Skill
A composable execution capability invoked by one or more agents. Skills are modular — they can be combined, sequenced, or run independently.

24 skills total. See SKILLS/ directory.

---

## PROGRAM_STATE.yaml
The single source of truth for a given program. Written by agents. Read by agents. Approved by the TPM.

Contains: metadata, milestones, risks, dependencies, decisions, contradictions, unresolved questions, evidence store, confidence scores.

---

## Review Packet
Output generated by the Governance Agent. Contains evidence, contradictions, confidence scores, and unresolved uncertainty. Requires TPM sign-off before any output is published.
